I’m usually the person who keeps asking “why?” until the problem starts making sense. I like learning how a business works, talking to the people using the product, and figuring out what actually needs to be designed.

I work best with a clear problem, open conversations, and room to explore. I’m comfortable working as a solo designer or as part of a team, depending on what the project needs.
